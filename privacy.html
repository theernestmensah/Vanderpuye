Privacy & data

Updated 20 November 2026

Signup preview

The preview lets you try the signup form using sample details. It asks for a name, phone number and region, with fields for constituency, role and a message. The page checks your entries in your browser. Finishing the preview clears the fields without creating a signup record or sending them to the campaign.

Cookies and site visits

The campaign page uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or browser storage for signup entries. Your browser requests pages, fonts and images from the web host. The host may process technical information such as your IP address and request time to deliver those files.

Photos, donations and external links

Photo downloads require no account. This site does not process payments. If you follow a link to a social network or news outlet, that service’s privacy policy applies there.

Questions about your data

Use the campaign contact links for privacy questions or to ask about information you shared directly with the campaign. Avoid posting your phone number or other personal details in a public comment.
