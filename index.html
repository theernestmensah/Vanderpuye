00The thesis

“Organisation decides everything, but organisation cannot survive when you have not built structures that will sustain it. We must rebuild that spirit.”

— Nii Lantey Vanderpuye, as reported by MyJoyOnline

He's not running to change the party. He says the party needs a reset of mind, a reset of heart, a reset of attitude, so the people who built it own it again.

Read the plan in three acts →
I

Mind · Heart · Attitude

Act One / 03

Reset.

A party its members are proud to own, with their voices heard and their dues paid willingly.

“…their decisions matter, their voices matter, their opinions matter, then they take ownership of the party.”GhanaWeb
  • 01Bring grassroots members into decision-making
  • 02Make formal membership and dues something people understand and want
II

Desk · Conference · Congress

Act Two / 03

Reform.

Proper structures for the people who serve: a welfare desk, and time to debate policy without the pull of an election.

“Why can't we have a desk at the national level that is responsible for the welfare of party members?”MyJoyOnline
  • 01A welfare desk at the National Secretariat for former MPs, DCEs, women organisers and officers
  • 02Separate policy conferences from elective congresses
  • 03A welfare system that is credible, sustainable and transparent
III

Resources · Momentum · 2028

Act Three / 03

Rebuild.

A party that pays its own way and keeps the momentum going into 2028.

“The NDC as an organisation can invest in its own people and create millionaires out of the party.”3News
  • 01Stop going round “with a cup in hand” to fund registration and agent training
  • 02Sustain the momentum built under the outgoing Chairman
  • 03The ambition: preferred party of two-thirds of Ghanaians within four years

“I want to lead not just to occupy an office, but to restore hope, protect our own and wipe away the tears of those who sacrificed for our party.”
